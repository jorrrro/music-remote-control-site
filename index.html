Music Remote Control

Music Remote Control lets you control music playing on your Mac from your iPhone, iPad, widgets, and Live Activities.

Connection Troubleshooting

Make sure your Mac and mobile device have Wi-Fi and Bluetooth enabled. Keep Music Remote Control open on the Mac while connecting.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. You can restore a previous Full Unlock purchase from the purchase window in the Mac app.
