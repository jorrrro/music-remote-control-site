Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Music Remote Control handles information when you use the macOS, iPhone, or iPad applications.

Information You Provide

Music Remote Control does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, address, contacts, or payment details.

Communication Between Devices

The Mac and mobile applications communicate directly over nearby network connections. Playback information, artwork, device status, and control commands may be transferred between your devices. This information is not routed through servers operated by the developer.

Crash and Diagnostic Information

Music Remote Control uses Firebase Crashlytics to help identify crashes and improve reliability. Crashlytics may collect diagnostic information such as crash reports, stack traces, app version, operating-system version, device model, and an installation identifier.

This diagnostic information is processed by Google as a service provider. Learn more from Firebase Privacy and Security .

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. The application receives transaction and entitlement information from Apple to determine whether features have been unlocked.

Tracking and Advertising

Music Remote Control does not display third-party advertising and does not use your information to track you across apps or websites.

Data Sharing

Information is not sold. Diagnostic information may be processed by Firebase Crashlytics solely to provide crash-reporting and application-reliability services.

Data Retention

Diagnostic information is retained only as needed to investigate crashes, maintain application reliability, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Children’s Privacy

Music Remote Control does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact joro.support@gmail.com .
